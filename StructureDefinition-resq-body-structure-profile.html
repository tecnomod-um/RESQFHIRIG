Resource Profile: RESQ BodyStructure Profile

Official URL: http://tecnomod-um.org/StructureDefinition/resq-body-structure-profile Version: 1.0.0
Active as of 2026-06-08 Computable Name: RESQBodyStructureProfile

BodyStructure profile for anatomical structures relevant to stroke, such as occluded arteries, stenosis sites and laterality-specific findings.

Allows observations and reports to reference a normalized anatomical site instead of repeating less structured body-site information.

Usages:

  • Refer to this Profile: Specific Finding Observation Profile
