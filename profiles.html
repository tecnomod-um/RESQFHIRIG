Conditions

Profile Why it exists
Stroke Diagnosis Condition Captures the index stroke diagnosis, onset, body site and stroke-specific classification details.
Stroke Risk Factor Condition Represents comorbidities and risk factors relevant to stroke quality measurement.
Post-Stroke Complication Condition Records complications occurring after the index stroke.

Observations

Profile Why it exists
Base Stroke Observation Shared subject, encounter, status and code constraints for registry observations.
Vital Sign Observation Blood pressure and other vital signs using components.
Functional Score Observation NIHSS, mRS, ASPECTS and other severity or outcome scales.
Glasgow Coma Scale Observation Specialized GCS score binding.
Specific Finding Observation Imaging, procedure and clinical findings such as mTICI or stenosis.
Timing Metric Observation Door-to-needle, onset-to-door and related process metrics.
Analytics Observation Laboratory and analytics values such as glucose, LDL and INR.
Fever Observation Fever indicator or temperature value.
Hyperglycemia Observation Hyperglycemia checks and values.

TIA Clinical Symptoms Observation TIA symptom indicators and durations.
Patient Ventilated Observation Ventilation indicator.
Three-Month Contact Mode Observation Contact mode used for follow-up outcome capture.
