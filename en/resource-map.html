Resource Map

Resource Map

The RES-Q IG models one registry case as a connected set of FHIR resources. The table below is the fastest route from a real registry concept to the resource profile that represents it.

Resource type What it represents Main profiles
Patient Pseudonymized patient anchor and coded sex/gender RESQ Patient
Encounter Index stroke admission and pathway context Stroke Encounter
Organization Contributing registry hospital or organization Stroke Registry Organization
Location Hospitalized location, department or care intensity RESQ Location, Hospitalized Location
Condition Diagnosis, risk factors and post-stroke complications Stroke Diagnosis, Risk Factor, Post-Stroke Complication
Observation Scores, vital signs, labs, imaging findings, timings and follow-up indicators Observation profiles
Procedure Imaging, reperfusion, screening, VTE prophylaxis and treatments Procedure profiles
DiagnosticReport Imaging reports and thrombectomy outcome reports Stroke Imaging Report, Mechanical Thrombectomy Report
BodyStructure Anatomical sites such as occluded arteries and laterality RESQ BodyStructure
MedicationStatement Medication use before stroke onset and adherence Prior MedicationStatement
MedicationRequest Medication prescribed at discharge Discharge MedicationRequest
MedicationAdministration Acute or post-acute medication administrations MedicationAdministration profiles
PractitionerRole Performer category for screening procedures RESQ PractitionerRole
Appointment Three-month follow-up appointment ​Follow-up Appointment
Communication Three-month contact modality and communication status Three-Month Communication

How to Navigate

Use Profiles when you know the FHIR resource type. Use Modeling Decisions when you need to understand why a registry field was placed in a given resource. Use Terminology when a profile binds an element to a generated ValueSet.

flowchart TD
  Case["Stroke registry case"] --> Patient["Patient"]
  Case --> Encounter["Encounter"]
  Encounter --> Conditions["Condition profiles"]
  Encounter --> Observations["Observation profiles"]
  Encounter --> Procedures["Procedure profiles"]
  Encounter --> Medications["Medication resources"]
  Procedures --> Reports["DiagnosticReport"]
  Reports --> Observations
  Observations --> BodyStructure["BodyStructure where anatomy matters"]
  Encounter --> FollowUp["Appointment / Communication"]
